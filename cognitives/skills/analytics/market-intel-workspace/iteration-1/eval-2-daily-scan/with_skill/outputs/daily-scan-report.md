# Daily Market Scan -- Capital Deployment Report
**Date:** March 30, 2026 | **Capital:** $50,000 | **Regime:** Elevated Volatility / Geopolitical Risk-On Rotation

## Executive Summary

The market is in a fear-driven dislocation. The Iran conflict has pushed oil above $113/bbl, VIX sits at 30.6, and the S&P 500 is 9% off its highs. Tech is getting crushed on rotation into energy and value. This is exactly the environment where patient capital earns outsized returns -- high-quality names are trading at multi-year lows on macro fear, not fundamental deterioration. The $50k should be deployed across three tiers: a high-conviction core in oversold quality tech, a tactical energy play riding the oil spike, and a speculative infrastructure/nuclear bet on structural AI demand.

## Macro Context

**Interest rates:** The Fed held at 3.50-3.75% in March, with the dot plot signaling at most one cut in 2026. The long-run neutral rate was revised upward to 3.0%. This is a "higher for longer" regime that compresses growth multiples but supports value/cyclical sectors.

**Geopolitical shock:** The 2026 Iran conflict is the dominant market variable. The Strait of Hormuz closure disrupted 20% of global oil supply, pushing Brent from ~$83 to $113+ in one month. Trump's pause on strikes against Iranian energy infrastructure triggered an 11% single-day oil decline on March 23, showing extreme sensitivity to diplomatic headlines.

**Economic cycle:** Mid-cycle expansion with stress. GDP growth at 2.4%, but PCE inflation revised up to 2.7% on energy pass-through. The "January Barometer" confirmed a massive rotation into value and cyclicals. Tech/growth is underperforming; energy, industrials, and infrastructure are leading.

**Volatility:** VIX at 30.6, well above the long-term average of ~20. The 52-week range of 13.4 to 60.1 tells you the market has been through genuine panic (likely early March when war broke out). Current levels suggest elevated fear but not capitulation -- this is the "uncomfortable but rewarding" zone for deploying capital.

**Bottom line:** Macro is a tailwind for energy and value, a headwind for unprofitable growth. But the best opportunities are in quality companies that got dragged down by the macro selloff despite intact fundamentals. That is where the $50k goes.
