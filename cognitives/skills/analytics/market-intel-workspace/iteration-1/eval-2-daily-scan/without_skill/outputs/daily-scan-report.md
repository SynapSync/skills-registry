## The Dominant Theme: Iran War & Oil Shock

The US-Iran conflict is now in its fifth week. This is the single biggest driver of market action right now:

- **Oil has exploded.** Brent crude is up ~55% in March alone -- a record monthly gain since the contract's 1988 inception. WTI closed above $100 for the first time since the conflict began. The IEA calls it the largest supply disruption in the history of the global oil market, with Strait of Hormuz flows collapsing from 20M bbl/day to nearly zero.
- **Houthi involvement** has expanded the theater and stoked fears of a prolonged confrontation.
- **Tech is getting hit hardest** (-1%+ on the day), while energy, defense, and select financials/utilities are holding up or gaining.
- **Falling Treasury yields** (10Y down to 4.34%) indicate a flight to safety and growing recession concerns.

## Risk Factors to Watch This Week (Mar 30 - Apr 3)

1. **Iran conflict escalation** -- any widening (Houthi involvement, additional Gulf state disruption) could push oil to $120+ and trigger broader equity selloff.
2. **Fed commentary** -- Powell spoke on inflation today. Any hawkish tilt in the face of oil-driven inflation could hit bonds and equities simultaneously.
3. **Q1 earnings season approaching** -- pre-announcements and guidance revisions in the next 2-3 weeks will set the tone for Q2.
4. **VIX above 30** -- historically, sustained VIX > 30 precedes either a capitulation low (buying opportunity) or further cascade. Size positions accordingly.
5. **Treasury curve dynamics** -- falling 10Y yields suggest recession pricing. Watch for 2Y/10Y spread moves.

## Bottom Line

The market is in a fear-driven regime dominated by the Iran oil shock. Energy and defense are the momentum trades.
